This privacy policy covers mrbell.dev and any SMS/text messaging programs
operated by Michael Bell in connection with it.

## Website

This site does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts. No personal
data is collected from visitors browsing this site.

## SMS Messaging Program

If you receive text messages as part of a messaging program operated by
Michael Bell, the following applies:

- Your mobile phone number and any information associated with the
  messaging program is used **only** to deliver the messages you have been
  enrolled to receive.
- Your mobile information **will never be shared or sold to third parties
  or lead generators** for any purpose, including marketing.
- Message and data rates may apply, as determined by your mobile carrier.
- To stop receiving messages, reply **STOP** at any time. Reply **HELP**
  for assistance.
